# Thornveil art, animation, and Bramble Crown combat

Thornveil now has a complete presentation pass across the Approach, Root Shrine, and Crown Glade.

## Artwork and animation

- The supplied Bramble Crown sheet was converted from a baked checkerboard JPG into a genuine transparent 4×5 PNG atlas.
- All twenty Crown poses are used for front and back movement, side movement, attack windups, charge motion, staggering, and defeat.
- Thornlings now use a dedicated four-state painted sheet for idle, hopping, striking, and defeat.
- An eight-piece transparent prop atlas adds thorn nests, chained hunter effigies, glowing root shrines, dead trees, bramble arches, spore clusters, antler totems, and eruption art.
- Thorn patches visibly grow and flare through warning and damage phases. Drifting green spores, pulsing props, and the painted northern gate keep the three rooms moving without hiding combat warnings.
- A new twelve-frame guardian atlas gives the Warden, Poacher, and Seer separate idle, movement, attack, and defeat art. Their silhouettes match their mechanics: root shield, antler bow, and glowing root magic.

## Authored area progression

- The Approach introduces shield pressure and thorn shots among three animated hazard patches.
- The Root Shrine combines a Warden line with a Seer who predicts the player's movement and marks a delayed root eruption.
- The Crown Glade expands to four hazard patches around the final arena and preserves clear escape lanes.
- Three optional environmental interactions—the chained oath, Root Shrine memory, and collar-sigil—tell how hunters created the Crown. Each awards 35 XP and persists in the save.
- Finding all three memories cracks the collar. The Crown still has attack armor, but committed attacks take 65% damage instead of 45%; stagger remains the safest damage window.
- Thornveil has its own 64-second stereo forest score plus original root, volley, and memory effects. Grass footsteps and combat cues transition automatically across all three rooms.

## Bramble Crown attacks

- **Gore:** a long antler charge with a clear line; sidestep it.
- **Rake:** a broad frontal claw cone; get behind the Crown.
- **Root Snare:** three predicted eruptions in phase one and five in phase two; keep moving between warning rings.
- **Thorn Volley:** five projectiles in phase one and seven in phase two; strafe across the fan rather than retreating in a straight line.
- **Crown Burst:** an expanding outer ring with a safe pocket close to the boss.
- **The Thicket Answers:** phase two summons Thornlings to pressure the arena.

The Crown's bark reduces damage while it commits to attacks. Its hide opens during stagger windows. Phase two begins at 55% health, speeds up the animation cadence, strengthens root and volley patterns, and adds a visible green crown aura.

Leaving the Crown Glade resets a living boss, its summoned Thornlings, projectiles, roots, status effects, and health. Defeat and the hunter cache remain saved.

## Verification

The Thornveil test covers room navigation, collision, all four painted atlases, the three bespoke guard roles, memory rewards, collar armor, original music routing, boss wake-up, unique state routing, root and volley spawning, thorn-ring damage, save persistence, and hunt completion. Presentation captures cover all three environments and both new attack telegraphs.
